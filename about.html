Our Story

Founded in 2010 by fitness enthusiasts Michael and Sarah Thompson, PowerFit Gym began with a simple mission: to create a welcoming environment where people of all fitness levels could achieve their health goals.

What started as a small studio with just a few pieces of equipment has grown into a state-of-the -art fitness center with multiple locations. Despite our growth, we've maintained our core values of personalized attention, community support, and evidence-based training methods.

Today, PowerFit Gym is proud to have helped thousands of members transform their lives through fitness, nutrition guidance, and unwavering support.

Our Mission & Values

Our Mission

To empower individuals to transform their lives through fitness, providing the tools, knowledge, and support needed to achieve lasting health and wellness.

Our Vision

To be the leading fitness community that inspires and enables people to live healthier, happier lives through accessible, innovative, and personalized fitness solutions.

Our Values

We believe in integrity, inclusivity, excellence, innovation, and community. These core values guide everything we do, from how we design our programs to how we interact with our members.
