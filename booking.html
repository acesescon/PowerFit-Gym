For your first session, please bring comfortable workout clothes, athletic shoes, a water bottle, and a small towel. If you have any medical conditions, please bring relevant information or documentation.

How do I reschedule my session?

We recommend arriving 10-15 minutes before your scheduled session. This allows time for changing, completing any necessary paperwork, and a brief orientation if it's your first visit.
